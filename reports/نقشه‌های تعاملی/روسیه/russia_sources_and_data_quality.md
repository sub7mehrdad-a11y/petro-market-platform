### Excluded / not verified
* **Berezniki Soda Plant (BSZ, Perm Krai)**: every fetched source describes soda ASH only (KP: up to 650 kt/yr soda ash, 600 kt made in 2022; Interfax Apr 2025: soda ash, owned 95.84% by BSK per end-2022 affiliates list; ura.news: soda ash; bsz.ru: "Кальцинированная сода — основной продукт"). ibprom lists "food soda" only for 1941-45 history. RAEX (2018) says BSK's complexes include Berezniki, and RIA (2018) lists Berezniki among "other soda producers", but neither says Berezniki makes bicarbonate. => Not marked on the map. URLs: https://www.interfax.ru/russia/1018194 , https://regions.kp.ru/perm/bereznikovskii-sodovyi-zavod/ , https://www.bsz.ru/ , https://ura.news/news/1052750317 , https://ibprom.ru/bereznikovskiy_sodovyy_zavod
* **Krymsky Soda (Crimea)**: named as a soda producer in the sano.elpub and RIA pages; Crimea is disputed territory and no bicarbonate evidence was found => excluded.
* Other candidates searched (Siberia/Altai: Kuchuksulfat = sodium sulfate; Achinsk, Pikalevo = soda ash as by-product of alumina; SDS Azot, Kuibyshevazot = by-product soda) are not bicarbonate producers per https://sano.elpub.ru/jour/article/download/281/272 and https://tcj.ru/wp-content/uploads/2013/12/2013_07-08_34-41_soda.pdf (the latter says Sterlitamak and Berezniki make commercial soda ash and that food soda is made by carbonation, naming no other producer).

Also OSRM does not route Derbent-Baku through Samur (it chose a route via Georgia, 1485 km), so the crossing may be treated as closed in OSM data; the map therefore ends the route on the Russian side.

Western land borders (Finland, Estonia, Latvia, Belarus) not assessed.

## 3. Distance method and the Kazakhstan-transit problem
OSRM public server (router.project-osrm.org, driving profile). `table` for the matrix (source = plant at 55.96,53.62; 2-decimal coordinates), `route?overview=simplified&geometries=geojson` for geometry. The raw shortest road for seven destinations passes through Kazakhstan, so these were re-routed with intermediate waypoints (Samara 50.15,53.20 / Saratov 46.03,51.53 / Volgograd 44.51,48.71 for the Caspian-Caucasus group; Yekaterinburg 60.60,56.84 / Tyumen 65.53,57.15 / Omsk 73.37,54.99 for the Siberian group) and the OSRM total distance of that route is stored in `dist`. All stored route polylines were checked vertex-by-vertex against the Natural Earth Russia polygon: all inside Russia except the final border-crossing vertices.

Not measured: rail (Russia's main freight mode), freight rates, border waiting, sanctions/payment issues, ship legs on the Caspian.

## 4. Data-quality problems and inconsistencies
1. **217 kt vs 193.9 kt**: spark.ru gives 217 kt food-soda output; Interfax gives 193.9 kt for 2024 output AND sales. Spark's year is not stated in the fetched text.
2. **Output above stated capacity**: 217 kt exceeds the 200 kt "production and packaging" capacity (Oct 2022). Either capacity grew after 2022 or the 217 kt figure is not comparable. Capacity treated as stale.
3. **84% market share** (2018) is an anonymous company-source claim reported by RIA; no independent check. Note that the same article's other soda producers (Berezniki, Crimea) are not shown to make bicarbonate, so 84% implies that someone else makes the remaining 16% (imports or another plant) - unidentified.
4. **Berezniki figures conflict**: KP says 600 kt soda ash made in 2022 and capacity "up to 650 kt"; bsz.ru shows a "production volume 12.6 mln t" (implausible for one plant; probably a cumulative or mislabeled figure). Kommersant (2009) said 500 kt/yr; KP also uses "AO BSK" and "AO BSZ" interchangeably. Irrelevant for bicarbonate but flagged.
5. **Press-release grade**: the 200 kt capacity comes from a paid/PR placement on rbc.ru (ufa.plus.rbc.ru/pressrelease).
6. **Sanctions-era data gap**: no 2025 bicarbonate-only production or export figure found; the Jul 2026 Interfax text speaks only about soda ash and bicarbonate together and company-wide utilisation (83.3%).
7. **Ownership/legal flux**: federal trust-management of 951,494 BSK shares (Russian Hydrogen / Bashkortostan), removal from strategic list in Mar 2025, 2020-21 deprivatisation dispute; ibprom page is a 2015 snapshot.
8. **Raw-material risk**: Shakhtau limestone was projected to run out around 2027 (2020 statement; 2018 statement said Dec 2022); alternative deposits reportedly "30 years" (https://www.interfax.ru/russia/832394). Continuity of output is therefore uncertain.
9. **Plant coordinate**: city-centre point; actual plant is in the industrial zone, location not confirmed (no source fetched).
10. **Samur**: OSRM does not route through the Samur crossing; checkpoint position approximate (+-10 km).
